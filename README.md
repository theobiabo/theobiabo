## About me

The best software feels simple because someone took the time to understand the hard parts. That is the kind of work I enjoy: finding clarity inside complex systems, making careful technical decisions, and leaving the product stronger than I found it.

I am a full-stack software engineer who works across the product, from user-facing experiences to the systems and infrastructure behind them. TypeScript and Rust are my tools of choice. I care about thoughtful interfaces, dependable services, maintainable architecture, and software that remains predictable under real-world pressure.

## What I bring

- **Full-stack product engineering:** cohesive experiences from interface to infrastructure
- **TypeScript:** modern web applications, services, tooling, and integrations
- **Rust:** safe, performant systems, services, and SDKs
- **Fintech:** ledger systems, payment infrastructure, and financial integrations
- **Open source:** reusable libraries, clear documentation, and collaborative development
